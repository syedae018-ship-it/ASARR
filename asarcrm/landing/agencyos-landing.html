Straight answers

Before AgencyOS enters your operation.

A serious operating system needs clear expectations about ownership, deployment, support, and unfinished work.

Is AgencyOS a SaaS subscription?+

No. The product direction is a licensed private installation for one agency. Hosting and external providers such as email or payment services may still have their own costs.

Is the full system available today?+

No. The CRM, lead forms, clients, projects, tasks, invoices, portfolio, and branding form the current MVP. The client portal, signatures, advanced finance, permissions, automation, and Docker packaging are roadmap modules.

Can it match our agency workflow?+

The target system supports configurable branding, roles, stages, templates, statuses, and workflow rules. Complex custom integrations are separately scoped implementation work.

Where will our data live?+

The current MVP uses Supabase for PostgreSQL data, authentication, row-level security, and file storage. It can connect to Supabase Cloud or a self-hosted Supabase deployment.

Does a one-time license include lifetime custom development?+

No. The license covers the agreed product edition. Installation, migrations, custom integrations, and ongoing support are separate unless explicitly included in the proposal.
